# v0.12.0 — Lead CRM + Export

Added a CRM layer for scraped/scored companies: lead lifecycle, search/filtering, tags, notes, activity history, assignee, score snapshot, bulk status changes and CSV export.

Endpoints: GET/POST/PUT `/api/leads`, GET `/api/leads/:id`, notes/tags endpoints, bulk status and `/api/leads/export.csv`.

The prototype uses in-memory storage; production migration should move leads and activities to PostgreSQL with indexes, pagination, RBAC and persistent score snapshots.
