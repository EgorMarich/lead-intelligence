# v0.11.0 — Lead Scoring Engine 2.0

Configurable organization-scoped scoring profiles are introduced.

Endpoints:
- GET `/api/lead-scoring/profiles`
- GET `/api/lead-scoring/profiles/:id`
- POST `/api/lead-scoring/profiles`
- PUT `/api/lead-scoring/profiles/:id`
- POST `/api/lead-scoring/profiles/:id/score`

Scores are normalized to 0–100:
- HOT 90–100
- WARM 70–89
- COLD 40–69
- LOW 0–39

The response includes matched reasons and missed opportunities.

The prototype stores profiles in memory. Production should persist them in PostgreSQL, version profiles, add audit history/RBAC, and support bulk scoring through the crawl queue.
