- Public `/` commercial landing page, separate from the authenticated app.

- Discovery/search demonstration.

- Product capability grid.
- AI Sales Manager showcase.

## Design

- Dark SaaS visual language inherited from v0.34.
- Lime primary accent, violet/cyan reserved for future product surfaces.
- Small corner radii, 1px borders, minimal shadows.
- Inter + selective Press Start 2P typography.
- Responsive layouts for desktop/tablet/mobile.
- No Tailwind and no UI framework introduced.

- `/` — public landing
