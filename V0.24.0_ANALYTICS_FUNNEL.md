# Lead Intelligence v0.24.0 — Analytics & Sales Funnel

## Added
- Tenant-scoped analytics API: `/api/v1/analytics/overview`
- Breakdowns: `/api/v1/analytics/breakdown?dimension=channel|city|category|source`
- Discovery metrics: jobs, found, processed, errors, processing rate
- Enrichment success/failure metrics
- CRM funnel: qualified, contacted, interested, negotiation, converted, lost
- Stage-to-stage conversion rates
- Outreach channel performance
- Lead `dealValue` and `revenue` for pipeline/revenue analytics
- Analytics dashboard with period selector (7/30/90 days)
- DB indexes for analytics workloads

## Notes
Funnel status counts are based on the current CRM status of leads created in the selected period. Historical stage events can be added later for cohort-level funnel attribution.
